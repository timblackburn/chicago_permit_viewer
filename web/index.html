Chicago Building Permits 1872–1954

3rd-party viewer for Chicago Building Permit microfilm

·

Look up a Chicago building permit in two steps:

  1. Search your street address below to open the 3×5 Street Index Card microfilm for your block (corner lots show both intersecting streets).
  2. Read the permit date or stamped Book & Page on the card (e.g. S. 47 Page 2), then use the Jump to Permit Ledger bar at the bottom of the viewer to open the original ledger entry.
